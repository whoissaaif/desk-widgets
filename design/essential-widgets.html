What we took from Apple

  1. One job per widget. Weather leads with the temperature and the high and low because that is what people check.
  2. Apple's sizes and spacing. Small 170 pt and medium 364 pt, so two smalls line up with one medium; 16 pt margins, tighter only inside grouped content.
  3. Readable at a glance. No text under 11 pt, the system font (Segoe UI Variable on Windows), big numbers for the one value that matters.
  4. Never color alone. System bars print their values beside them; finished reminders get a strike-through as well as a fill.
  5. Three appearances. Light, dark, and a vibrant monochrome for when the desktop is behind other windows.
  6. Refresh only as often as the data changes. Time ticks each second, weather every 15 minutes, the photo once an hour, notes only on edit. That is also how the app stays near zero CPU.
