Live in this page: session times and countdowns (real Mexico City GP weekend, shown in your time zone), lap ticker. Sample data: standings, points, results and live gaps. They're placeholders, not real 2026 results.

How they differ.

1Race hubLarge

Everything at once: next race, the five sessions, live countdown, top five. The one to pick if someone adds only one F1 widget. Needs a new large card size (364 × 382 pt).

2Race weekendMedium

The schedule answer: "when's qualifying?" Finished sessions dim, the next one turns red, and it switches to "Live" while a session runs.

3CountdownSmall

One number: time to lights out. The track outline makes it read as F1 without a logo.

4Your driverSmall

Pick a driver once; see position, points and the gap to the leader. Most personal of the set.

5Driver standingsMedium

Top five with points and gap. Quiet between races, updates only after a session is classified.

6Live timingMedium

During a session: lap count, top five, interval and tyre. Turns into the standings widget when nothing is running.

7ConstructorsMedium

Team points as bars, so the shape of the season reads at a glance. Points printed beside every bar.

8PodiumSmall

Last race's top three on steps. Mostly a Monday-morning widget.

9Title fightSmall

The gap between first and second, and how many points are left to play. Best late in the season.
